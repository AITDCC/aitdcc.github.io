Official dataset release

The complete AIT Data Compression Challenge 2026 dataset consists of 16 files, A–P. Files A–H form the Training dataset and files I–P form the Testing dataset. The Testing dataset was hidden during the competition and is now publicly released for reproducibility.

Canonical files: use the individual files linked on this page for byte-for-byte reproduction of the benchmark. The previous data/data.zip is the legacy Training archive containing A–H only and should not be interpreted as the complete A–P dataset.

Reproducibility, commercial validation & attribution

The publicly released AITDCC 2026 files A–P may be used for independent reproducibility studies, lossless-compression benchmarking, scientific evaluation, and commercial-validation reports. Such use does not imply endorsement of any compressor, product, company, or reported result by the AITDCC organizers.

  • Mandatory attribution: any use of one or more AITDCC files must clearly identify the data as originating from the AIT Data Compression Challenge 2026 (AITDCC 2026) and link to the official benchmark page at https://aitdcc.github.io/.
  • Paper citation: publications, technical reports, benchmark reports, commercial-validation reports, presentations, and other public uses of the dataset must also cite the associated AITDCC paper: “The 2026 Algorithmic Information Theory Data Compression Challenge”.
  • Source provenance: when reporting results for individual files, users should retain and acknowledge the original provenance/source of the corresponding file whenever that provenance is provided by AITDCC or by the underlying source dataset.
  • Reproducibility: report the exact files evaluated (for example, A–H, I–P, or A–P) and verify them against the SHA-256 values published here.
  • Results: independently obtained results should be described as independent evaluations, not as official AITDCC competition results.
  • Third-party rights: where any underlying source material is subject to separate third-party attribution, citation, or licensing terms, those terms continue to apply.

In short, reuse is permitted for reproducibility, research, benchmarking, and commercial validation, provided that the AITDCC origin is credited, the associated AITDCC paper is cited, and any applicable third-party source terms are respected.
